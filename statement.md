Problem Statement
Students and professionals frequently struggle to track their attendance accurately, often leading to unexpected academic penalties or workplace compliance issues. Manual tracking is tedious and prone to errors, while many institutional systems do not provide forward-looking insights. Users lack a predictive tool that tells them exactly how many future classes or days they can miss—or must attend—to maintain their target attendance percentage.
Scope of the Project
The project scope encompasses the design, development, and deployment of a web-based Attendance Calculator application.
In-Scope:
• User authentication and secure profile creation.
• Dashboard for managing multiple courses, subjects, or work modules.
• Core calculation engine for current percentages and predictive "what-if" scenarios.
• Mobile-responsive front-end interface.
• Local data caching for offline accessibility.
Out-of-Scope:
• Direct integration with official university or corporate LMS/ERP databases (e.g., Canvas, Banner).
• Biometric or geolocation-based automated check-ins.
Target Users
• College and University Students: Individuals managing strict institutional attendance policies (e.g., maintaining a mandatory 75% threshold) across multiple classes.
• Working Professionals: Employees tracking shift attendance, remote work days, or billable hours to comply with HR policies.
• Academic Advisors: Educators who need a quick tool to help students calculate recovery paths for low attendance.
High-Level Features
• Multi-Subject Dashboard: Allows users to add, edit, and view individual attendance statuses for multiple courses simultaneously.
• Predictive "Absence Calculator": Computes the exact number of upcoming sessions a user can safely miss without dropping below their target percentage.
• Recovery Planner: Calculates the consecutive number of classes a user must attend to restore their status if they are currently below the threshold.
• Visual Status Indicators: Employs color-coded alerts (Green/Yellow/Red) to instantly communicate compliance status.
• Smart Reminders: Generates daily or weekly notifications prompting users to update their attendance logs.
